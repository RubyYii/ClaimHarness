## Source Problem

# Cultural Archive Interpretation Alignment

I want to evaluate whether multimodal AI outputs can support cultural archive interpretation rather than only recognizing visible objects. The domain problem involves visible item details, catalog notes, metadata, provenance context, missing information, and expert interpretation boundaries.

The risk is that the AI task becomes generic captioning. Object recognition and caption similarity are not enough because a visible element may only become meaningful when aligned with catalog notes, contextual records, and expert review.

## Domain Goal

Evaluate whether AI outputs align visible archive details, catalog notes, source context, and expert interpretation boundaries.

## Not Allowed Goal

object-only captioning as expert interpretation

## Meaningful Outputs

- Region-note alignment evidence
- Interpretation fidelity notes
- Uncertainty-aware interpretation candidates

## Non-Meaningful Outputs

- Generic object captioning
- Authoritative interpretive claims without source or expert support
